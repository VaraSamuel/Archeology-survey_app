ANIMAL HUSBANDRY –		1994-95


HERDING – 1 family is doing sheepherding; 60-70 sheep, 500 goats
		Goats eat scrub oak (ardıç), even through snow. 
		Goat is called “şeytan”, meaning a bit playful in a surprising way.

		This region gets 2-3 meters of snow; at 1 meter goats go out to graze.

Shepherd or owner takes herd to yayla over the Çile mountain (peak 1,600 m.) for summer; in winter they reurn to the village. They are fed: hay, barley, rye.

They also hunt, partridge (keklik), rabbit, wolf, fo, sansar (polecat), bıldırcın (quail).

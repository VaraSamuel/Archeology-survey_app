We are across from Beˆerkaç Tepesi. 
(The word “Erkaç” means place where herd stays, “yayıldı©ı yer”.
So, these 5 contiguous hills are where the herds used to stay in the past.
Beˆerkaç is c. 4 km south of the village. We came to a “nadas” field, there used to be a fold in this field..nothing left. Until 1950’s most of the farmland was MERA. We are at 912 m., lots of stony land.
Karahöyük is NE of us; HT is to the north. In this non-existent fold, there were c. 1,000 sheep.
Other fold locations: Çile Da©, O©lanölen, Tül Tepe,Çatak, Karahöyük--none of these, except at KH, is in use.

LANDUSE, GRAZING zones: 
1) highland, mountain top and high plateau (1200 m). e.g. Beˆerkaç 
(c. 3.5-4 km south of village proper), Türbe areas (c. 4 km N from village);
2) village environs (abutting agricultural zones)
3) marshland, no agriculture, lots of reeds, salt marshes where the sheep love to pick out the young reed shoots which are juicy and salty, it makes them drink water-- healthy..this zone is surrounding  Karahöyük, at a distance of c. 500 m., to 2 km. to the east ande south.


28.6.00
Going to HT, near Karahöyük.. Passed by Beyce©iz, Ya©murbaba, Kocali, HT. We observe on the way, that part of the soil which is heavily cultivated with wheat and barley, is very dark brown..looks healthy.
The crops are long and almost yellow. Two kinds of wheat: Rus and one with  awns “kılçık”...barley is ready to be harvested. Sunflower is very widely grown. 
Karahöyük hamlet-- only 2 families- Hulusi said that his brother and he inherited this land at KH from their father. There are some one-storey residential buildings, cow pen, barn, storage areas for agricultural equipment, cow shed and a large a©ıl. The number of buildings are too many for a 2 HH hamlet. 
At the foot of the mound is  an old fold KH-2, which Hulusi and father rebuilt, it consists of a large “salak” connected to a roofed area. All walls are local stone and mudbrick. Both open and closed area dimensions are c. 30x35 sq m. They own 350-400 sheep which are herded by a çoban. In winter the men of the two HH stay in KH while the women and children go to HT. The men like to stay near their animals. In front are veg. gardens, fruit trees and beyond are wheat, barley, sunflower fields. There is a small tea garden, to the right of it is a fenced-in area where an excavation house and storage area were built in the ‘70s when Burhan Tezcan excavated at KH..The excavated area is small, to the west of the mound; Roman levels..This höyük is multi-period, earliest is EBA. The rails and the old iron wheelbarrows are still intact on top of the mound.
On southern edge of the mound is an abandoned, old fold which we drew, KH-1. (for description, see Fold Survey Transcrip)

 Where were the folds? Kavaın Kırma, Muratoglu, Kepezler, Öz, Acıpınar mevkileri... Current farmland used to be grazing..after tapulama, they became farmland.

Where were the fields in the past? near Sakarya. (it is 500 m. From village) Got water with arks..About 2-3 km. from village. How do you do it now? WITH motor and electricity. Most of the land is unirrigated.. we also don’t do much Nadas, because land is little in proportion to population.

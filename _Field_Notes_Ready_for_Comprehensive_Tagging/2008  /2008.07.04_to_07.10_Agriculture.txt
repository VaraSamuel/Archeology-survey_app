Agriculture –						4.7.08

The barley has already been harvested about 2 weeks ago. The wheat is half harvested, with big combines that cut the stalk, make the hay into bales and 
store the wheat in its compartment. The whole process of 3 tasks takes very short time.

The herds are let into the harvested fields to get the kernels on the ground- at least it makes up for the scarcity of grass for the herds. Though Duran said that herds can find smallest plants and dig them out where the human eye cannot detect them.

10.7.08  SÖ

10.7.08

Went to SÖ this morning to check on changes in Land Use, agriculture , herding etc..
My former informant Ismail Üstün (80) died 3 yrs. ago. Hasan Güngör family moved to Polatli. Hüseyin Öztoprak, the man who was in charge of the Irrigation to SÖ is still around—but I did not see him.

My informant was a younger man, Mehmet Güney, who supervises the Petrol stand at the entrance to the village. He was very pessimistic about the present and the future of agriculture on a national level. As in ÇK, he talked about the high cost of fuel, fertilizer, machinery etc…and the cheap price given by the buyers for crops—especially the State. Also, the lack of knowledge of the farmer about seeds, fertilizer, bugs that eat crops etc…and Ilçe Tarim or other Agricultural Engineers who work for the Gov’t should make the rounds to teach the farmer…
LAND:  has 70,000 d. of land. ½ is grazing, ½ is for agriculture. (8,000 is irrigated). Large portion is dry farming.
Herding: Mera- c. 35,000 dönüm; 
Agriculture – 8,000 (irrigated); Dry agri. ( c. 25,000 d.)
POP: c. 1000, 150 HH.
ANIMALS: altogether 10,000 (c. 300-500 goats)—In 1995 (4,000 sh.& g)

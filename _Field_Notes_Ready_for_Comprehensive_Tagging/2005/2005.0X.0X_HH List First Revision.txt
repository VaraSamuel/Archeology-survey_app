

HOUSEHOLDS. 

Archaeologists do not excavate HH, they find the material remains of dwellings.
The HH is a unit of economic and social cooperation; it does not necessarily live under a single roof—co-residence is possible, but not a necessary element of  a HH.
Size and organization are linked to wider economic and subsistence spheres
These factors must be the source of inferences about causes of the variation in past societies.

*Notes: Household is defined as an individual house, with family/ies living in it. More than 1 HH may be located in the same courtyard.
One of those families, the oldest male, is generally the head of the HH. 
Ex.1: Father M. Ali Utgu and son Z. Utgu are in the same CY; Z’s older brother lives with his family at father’s house temporarily in the summer. He has a permanent house in Polatlı. Father is the head of the HH, all 3 HHs share income and expenditure (corporate HH).
Ex.2: Zekai & Recai Köseoglu live in same CY, in diff. Houses;
Parents live in another CY. They are a corporate HH; Father is head. 
Ex.3: Karagöz and Yavuz families live in same CY; 2 brothers are corporate, 1 older brother is independent. In time, each will be indep.

Fatma Caglar (Kürt Fatma) - 24/7/95“Yas kırk, is cılk”.....Haymana....is with his 3rd wife...She came to YH, ca. 35-40 yrs. ago. She lives in the last house, above Kezban & Muharrem Atiktig’s house, to the north of the village. She is 75 yrs. old, Kurdish, from a village of Haymana.. Her father is 112 years old ....is with his 3rd wife...She came to YH, ca. 35-40 yrs. ago. FATMA has also married 3 husbands.	Now she has 15 Karaman sheep (cared by Hasan Yaman) and 2 cows...Produce is only enough for her family and guests.Third husband, Dursun Caglar,  is from Alaca Höyük, Corum, had been working at excavations there, also worked as a shepherd.  He is about 50-55, ca. 20-25 years younger than she. Married for about 25-30 years. They have 3 children. One is called Emine Belge (Ramazan Belge)....All the girls are married, they live in the same courtyard, in houses that Fatma built.Now, he works as a shepherd at Ücret Köy, takes various peoples’ sheep together....stays with an Agha who pays him a salary....Part of payment is by products of sheep, part of it is cash.... All of his herds are Karaman, there is no Merinos there. He is gone for 6 months at a time. Comes back in winter. Last year he worked at excavations.“Kocanı özledin mi?”  “Özlesen de ne yapacan, elin isi, ...el isi,  istedigin zaman gelemez ki....” Alıstırdım kendimi.....We rent from Treasury, 50 d. land, and plant wheat and barley...unirrigated (at Kayran and Kabayazı). Half is wheat, half is barley.I hire someone, pay him and get the produce...We take wheat and barley for our needs, sell the extra.“My first husband and I worked very very hard...I took the kids to the field, some were born in the field, one fell into Sakarya, but was saved...Fatma has 10 children, 15 siblings....father is not too interested in me.My mother, his first wife, had “kuma” from the east...he brought the second one when he was doing Military service.. and one kid, plus one on the way...But she died while giving birth, so mother brought up those kids too... Half is wheat, half is barley.brought the second one After my mother died, he married the third one. He sold off everything, land and animals, and married the third one. Now he lives in Haymana, his two sons and in-laws take care of him.	Now I still work in the fields, for other people. I have high blood pressure, but otherwise I am healthy, I want to work....Köy Bahcesi: Rented from village...produce is sold, rent is paid...Each year, it is put on “ihale” by Muhtar... person who bids highest rental gets it...it is large, ca. 20 d. My first husband was my first cousin (aunt’s husband)...only stayed for 5 months with him...no children...went to my father’s, he beat me up, but I STILL DID NOT GO BACK... I did not have a child by him, I was only 13.We did not get along...With the second one, I had 7 children (Ekremin babası); with the third one, I had three kids....			(TO CONTINUE........
